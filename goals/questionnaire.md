### General
1. Across all four goals combined, how much time realistically exists for
   this in a normal week right now?
2. Is there one goal that most needs attention this month, or should all
   four be weighted evenly for now?
3. Any current external constraint — a busy stretch at work, travel,
   anything else — that should lower expectations short-term rather than
   being read later as a slip?

### Driving theory test
4. Current study method — the app you already have, a book, practice
   tests, nothing started yet?
5. Rough sense of where you are: just starting, partway through, nearly
   ready?
6. Realistic weekly study hours, given everything else?

### Sewing pattern development
7. What stage is the current project at — drafting, toile/muslin,
   fitting, sewing the final piece?
8. What counts as progress week to week for you — hours worked, a
   specific milestone, something else?
9. Is there a deadline driving this (an event, a date you want to wear
   it by), or is it genuinely open-ended right now?

### Fitness
10. What does the current calisthenics practice look like — frequency,
    current focus?
11. Realistic weekly frequency given everything else right now?

### Reading
12. What are you currently reading, or planning to start next?
13. Is the goal volume (a number of books), consistency (regular reading
    time), or both?

### System-level
14. Preferred time of day for the daily push.
15. Preferred day and time for the weekly check-in (separate from the
    daily push — pick a time you're likely to actually have a few minutes,
    since this one asks a couple of questions back).
16. Per-goal slip threshold — how many days of silence on a goal before it
    should be flagged? These can differ: a bounded goal like the theory
    test may warrant a shorter fuse than an open-ended one like sewing.
