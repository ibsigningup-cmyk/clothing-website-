喫茶店 — Kissaten

A room tuned
for listening.

Born of post-war Tokyo, the kissaten was a sanctuary: dim lamps, deep armchairs, records turned loud enough to feel. Ours is cut from the same cloth — a hideaway inside a 17th-century convent, reimagined for slow evenings and properly-mastered vinyl.

Expect a custom analogue sound system, a shelf of rare pressings, and Portugal's largest collection of whisky — over one hundred bottles, with a library of revered Japanese labels at its heart.
